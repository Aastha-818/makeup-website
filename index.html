Where Beauty Meets Artistry

Nidhi Kansal is Bangalore's premier luxury bridal makeup artist, celebrated for her ability to create looks that feel effortlessly authentic to each bride — whether North Indian, South Indian, NRI, or multi-cultural.

With over 78 glowing reviews and a 96% recommendation rate, Nidhi uses cutting-edge AI skin analysis technology to personalise every look. Her sweatproof, long-lasting formulas stay flawless from sunrise ceremonies to midnight receptions.

Based in RR Nagar, Bangalore, Nidhi travels outstation and serves NRI brides across India and internationally — bringing her signature blend of luxury cosmetics, hairstyling, and warmth to every occasion.
